Material & Atmosphere

Each project reflects a dialogue between material and atmosphere — using soil, stone, concrete, and wood as active elements that influence movement and perception. I’m drawn to the simplicity of form and the purity of aesthetic expression, seeking clarity through proportion, light, and texture.

Selected Work

This portfolio presents selected architectural works, drawings, and process documentation in a format that highlights both the conceptual and sensory dimensions of design. It aims to make spatial experience tangible, revealing how architecture emerges from the dialogue between human gesture, material texture, environmental condition, and the integration of aesthetic restraint.
